<b>About Me</b><br>
I've accepted a new position at the <a href="https://www.mcla.edu">Massachusetts College of Liberal Arts</a> as an
    Assistant Professor in Computer Science and Mathematics that starts in fall semester for the 2026-2027 academic
    year. Currently, I'm a Visiting Assistant Professor in the [Department of Computer Science at Williams College](https://csci.williams.edu).

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Department of Mathematics at the University of Pittsburgh](https://www.mathematics.pitt.edu), working with [Dr. Jonathan Rubin](https://www.mathematics.pitt.edu/people/jonathan-rubin). Our research focused on modeling the substantia nigra pars reticulata (SNr), an output nucleus of the basal ganglia which is implicated in Parkinson's Disease. For more detailed information about my academic and research endeavors, please explore the various tabs on this site.

***
<b>Background</b><br>
I earned my PhD in Applied Mathematics from the [University of New Hampshire](https://ceps.unh.edu/integrated-applied-mathematics), where I was mentored by [Dr. Kevin Short](https://findscholars.unh.edu/display/kmshort). My doctoral research centered on stabilizing chaotic dynamics in neural models. These results have been published and links to those papers can be found at [Publications](/publications/). 

I completed my undergraduate studies [Elon University](https://www.elon.edu/u/academics/arts-and-sciences/physics/) where I earned a BS in Physics.
