<b>About Me</b><br>
I am starting as an Assistant Professor in Mathematics and Computer Science at the <a href="https://www.mcla.edu">Massachusetts College of Liberal Arts</a> this fall semester (Fall 2026). Previously, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor in the [Department of Computer Science at Williams College](https://csci.williams.edu).

Before begininng my faculty positions, I was a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Department of Mathematics at the University of Pittsburgh](https://www.mathematics.pitt.edu), working with [Dr. Jonathan Rubin](https://www.mathematics.pitt.edu/people/jonathan-rubin). Our research focused on modeling the substantia nigra pars reticulata (SNr), an output nucleus of the basal ganglia which is implicated in Parkinson's Disease. For more detailed information about my academic and research endeavors, please explore the various tabs on this site.

***
<b>Background</b><br>
I earned my PhD in Applied Mathematics from the [University of New Hampshire](https://ceps.unh.edu/integrated-applied-mathematics), where I was mentored by [Dr. Kevin Short](https://findscholars.unh.edu/display/kmshort). My doctoral research centered on stabilizing chaotic dynamics in neural models. These results have been published and links to those papers can be found at [Publications](/publications/). 

I completed my undergraduate studies [Elon University](https://www.elon.edu/u/academics/arts-and-sciences/physics/) where I earned a BS in Physics.
